6. Hierarchical Reconciliation Methods
✨ MinT Shrink (Default)
Method: Minimizes total forecast variance under hierarchical coherency constraint (Wickramasuriya et al. 2019).
  • Ledoit-Wolf covariance shrinkage
  • Optimal for most hierarchies
  • Computationally efficient
⬆️ Bottom-Up
Method: Sums leaf-level forecasts to parents with no adjustment.
  • Simple, no bias
  • No correction to leaves
  • Risk: leaf errors compound
⚖️ WLS & OLS
Methods: Weighted/Ordinary Least Squares for reconciliation.
  • WLS: Diagonal covariance weighting
  • OLS: Equal weights
  • Balanced approach
📊 Top-Down (Legacy)
Method: Prophet aggregate + proportional scaling.
  • Historical proportions
  • Simple implementation
  • Less optimal than MinT
🎯 Reconciliation Flow: Base forecasts (per-key) → Summing matrix (hierarchy) → Reconciliation (selected method) → Coherent forecasts (parent/child consistency guaranteed)
